\section{Cross-Application Case Studies}
\label{sec:cases}
\subsection{Indoor: Localization to Articulated Measurement}
Use the same sequence and total budget to switch between person location and elbow angle or movement amplitude. Inspect changes in evidence retrieval and reference coverage while checking that the person geometry does not change arbitrarily with the request. Report unresolved ambiguity as well as successful refinement.

\subsection{Automotive: Near-Field Coverage to Sparse-Target Support}
Use a driving observation with reliable nearby geometry and a sparse distant target. Compare generic densification and task-directed allocation, including target existence and false-positive checks. Required context can include ground and nearby structures; background is not automatically discarded.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
The case studies illustrate one common formulation at different spatial scales. Joint training, zero-shot cross-device transfer, and one-weight-model operation remain separate empirical questions.
