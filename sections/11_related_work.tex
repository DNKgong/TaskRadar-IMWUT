\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Radar front ends and generation.}
Contrast mmPC's temporal CFAR and OmniPC's joint physical-dimension processing with learned spectrum/point generation~\cite{mmpc2025,omnipc2026,rald2026,mmwaveflow2026}. They supply strong baselines; none should be dismissed as having no temporal or physical reasoning.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and task-driven enhancement.}
RaUF models anisotropic spatial uncertainty~\cite{rauf2026}. DiffRefine already densifies proposal regions for detection~\cite{diffrefine2025}. Our proposed distinction is application-goal composition and risk-aware scheduling of temporal evidence and geometry, not the existence of uncertainty outputs or task losses.

\paragraph{Language, geometry, and sensing goals.}
RadarLLM aligns radar tokens and language; LiDARCrafter uses language for controllable LiDAR synthesis~\cite{radarllm2026,lidarcrafter2026}. MetaQueries motivates the connector interface~\cite{metaqueries2025}. A measurement request differs from a scene description that supplies object state. Goal-oriented inverse problems provide a relevant conceptual foundation~\cite{goalved2023}.

\paragraph{Author limitations versus our scope analysis.}
RadarLLM explicitly discusses device-specification shifts and missing environment/human-object context. Other gaps in our comparison are inferred from published methods and evaluations, not asserted to be author-written admissions. Recheck primary sources and publication status before submission.
